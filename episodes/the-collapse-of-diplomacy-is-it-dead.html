The Collapse of Diplomacy - Is It Dead?

Follow on Spotify → · Follow on Apple Podcasts →

Diplomacy is broken. Missiles fly before messages arrive. Leaders bypass diplomats to tweet threats. Narratives matter more than negotiations. In this breakdown, we explore why traditional diplomacy—embassies, backchannels, quiet agreements—is dying in 2026. We look at ongoing conflicts (Ukraine, Gaza, Iran war, US-China tensions) through the lens of Kumar's framework: Is diplomacy actually dead, or has it just gone underground? When does it work? Why does it fail? And who really holds power anymore—the diplomat or the storyteller? From the Cuban Missile Crisis to the Iran nuclear deal collapse, we trace what's changed and what comes next.
