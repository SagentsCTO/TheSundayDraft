How to Master Executive Presence & Workplace Conflict with Dan Messinger

Follow on Spotify → · Follow on Apple Podcasts →

How do you build true executive presence when the corporate ladder feels built on defensiveness and ego?

In this episode of The Sunday Draft, we sit down with Dan Messinger, an elite leadership development consultant and conflict resolution expert who has spent decades transforming workplace cultures inside $5B corporations.

Dan shares his battle-tested framework for managing workplace conflict, breaking down how to raise major concerns without blame and how leaders can listen without getting defensive. If you want to scale your leadership communication skills, master emotional intelligence, and stop complacency from quietly eroding your career and personal life, this conversation is essential listening.

What We Cover in This Episode:

  • The 4 Pillars of Executive Presence: Presence, Inner Power, Authenticity, and Connection.
  • The Power of Vulnerability: Why it is the single most overlooked skill leaders need to actually move people.
  • Disruption as a Launchpad: How a corporate layoff became the springboard for Dan’s elite consulting practice.
  • The Narcissistic Organization: Why the most dangerous thing a company can do is assume it already has all the answers.
