Can Art Survive War?

Follow on Spotify → · Follow on Apple Podcasts →

War doesn't just destroy lives — it erases culture, art, and centuries of human heritage. In this episode of The Sunday Draft, we examine how conflict, political corruption, and government decisions under Trump and Modi are putting art and cultural heritage at risk — and what is lost when culture becomes collateral damage.
