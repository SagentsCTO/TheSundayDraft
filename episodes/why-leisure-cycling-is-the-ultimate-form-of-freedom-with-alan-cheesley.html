Show Notes & Summary

We’ve all seen them: the cyclists tucked low over their handlebars, staring straight down at the asphalt, pushing their bodies to the absolute limit just to shave a few seconds off a digital leaderboard.

But what if the real magic of cycling happens when you actually stop to “smell the roses”?

In this episode of The Sunday Draft, host Raj sits down with Alan Cheesley, the mastermind behind Cycle4Fun UK. Alan is an ex-rugby player whose body has taken its fair share of punishment, but around his mid-fifties, he looked around at his friends accepting an unfit, sedentary lifestyle and decided it was time to change the narrative. He grabbed a small crew, put them through a casual training regimen, and surprised them with a trip that crossed right over the Alps.

Alan joins the show to share his deeply refreshing philosophy on non-competitive, leisure cycling. They break down the essential, unwritten “rules of the road”—including why you must never leave the last rider alone and how a group of varying physical abilities can seamlessly conquer 50-mile mountain climbs together.

Alan also dives into the practical logistics of modern bike travel. He explains how he used a smart Swytch conversion kit to turn his favorite regular frame into an airline-flyable e-bike system to accommodate a recent heart condition, and why the hidden backroads of Bosnia, Croatia, and Puglia offer some of the safest, most welcoming, and breathtaking cycling trails in the world.

Key Takeaways From the Episode:

  • The Last-Rider Rule: The simple camaraderie framework that ensures no one gets left behind or feels isolated on a tough climb.
  • Saving Your Knees: Why shifting down a gear and focusing on fluid “pedal circles” beats forcing raw muscle power every single time.
  • Flying With an E-Bike: How to legally bypass airline battery restrictions by splitting a converted frame layout between hand luggage and checked baggage.
  • Leisure vs. Competition: Transitioning away from high-intensity stats toward community-driven, affordable bike tours featuring local food, personal backup vans, and zero ego.
