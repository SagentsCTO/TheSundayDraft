Why Highly Articulate Leaders Still "Shrink" Under Pressure

Follow on Spotify → · Follow on Apple Podcasts →

On this week’s episode of The Sunday Draft, we sit down with Douglas Brewer—former corporate HR director turned elite communication coach based in Spain. Douglas works exclusively with C1+ fluent English speakers in European boardrooms who have the vocabulary but lose their presence under pressure.

He breaks down exactly why language craft and mental presence are two completely separate problems that require two completely different fixes.

🎧 Listen now on your platform of choice:

Key Takeaways From the Episode:

  • The Presence Mismatch: Why assumptions, not skill gaps, are the real reason fluent executives fade into the background during crucial meetings.
  • The Monologue vs. Conversation: Why delivering a structured presentation is mechanically and psychologically harder than back-and-forth dialogue—and how to train for it.
  • Strategic Silence: Practical lessons from Toastmasters’ “table topics” on how to lean into silence to regain control of a room.
  • The AI Co-Pilot: How artificial intelligence fits into executive communication development without replacing the deep preparation that actually builds human confidence.
