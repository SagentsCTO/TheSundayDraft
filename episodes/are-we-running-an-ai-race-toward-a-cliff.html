Are We Running an AI Race Toward a Cliff?

Follow on Spotify → · Follow on Apple Podcasts →

With tools like Claude Code allowing developers and entrepreneurs to build features and MVPs in mere minutes, the playing field has officially leveled. Anyone can create anything instantly.

But with great power comes great responsibility.

In this episode of The Sunday Draft, we sit down with Silicon Valley tech veteran and executive coach Nader Vasseghi to discuss his new book, Conscious Creation. Nader breaks down a worrying trend in tech: machine intelligence is accelerating at an unprecedented rate, but human consciousness is falling behind.

We dive deep into why building software out of pure ego, fear, or image-building creates toxic corporate cultures and long-term societal chaos. Plus, we explore a fascinating, hidden dynamic happening right now in workplaces: how employees are using AI as a secret third-party coach to evaluate and react to their leaders in real-time.

Whether you are a developer, founder, or creator trying to find your purpose in a rapidly changing technical landscape, Nader’s 3-part framework for conscious leadership provides the exact map you need to step off the hamster wheel and build with true intent.

Key Takeaways From the Episode:

  • The “Ego-Compensatory” Trap: Why chasing fame and fortune through rapid product building leads to personal burnout and broken relationships.
  • The Cloud Shutdown Scenario: A thought experiment on our growing emotional and cognitive dependency on AI agents.
  • The 3 Pillars of Conscious Leadership: How to transition from a “lean startup” mentality to “abundance entrepreneurship” by leading from within, leading with others, and leading with impact.
