Build Wealth in Real Estate with a W2 Job | Andrew Postell

Follow on Spotify → · Follow on Apple Podcasts →

Is it possible to build generational wealth through real estate without quitting your day job?

In this episode of The Sunday Draft, Raj sits down with Andrew Postell—a veteran real estate investor with 25 years of hands-on experience and 15 years in lending. Andrew shares how he built a multi-million-dollar property portfolio while holding a full-time W2 job, doing just one to two transactions a year.

We dive deep into why challenging economic environments—like 9/11, the 2008 housing crash, and today’s high-rate market—are actually the best times for buyers to get in. Andrew breaks down the mechanics of house hacking, the BRRRR method (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat), zero-out-of-pocket hard money strategies, finding off-market deals through local investor groups, and when it makes sense to hold vs. consolidate assets into index funds.

In this episode

• Why tough housing markets favor buyers over sellers

• House hacking and using first-time buyer programs

• Demystifying the BRRRR strategy (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat)

• Why hard money beats conventional lending for acquiring off-market deals

• Finding reliable contractors and off-market deals in local investor groups

• How Andrew attends 40+ concerts a year through VetTix

Notable quotes

“Real estate is very boring, very slow, and very methodical—but it works. It is not a get-rich-quick scheme.” — Andrew Postell

“Real estate is forgiving. If you keep it long enough, it will forgive you.” — Andrew Postell

“When it’s easy, anybody can do it. The harder it is, the more opportunity there is for you to hone your skills.” — Andrew Postell
