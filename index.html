About the show

The Sunday Draft is an independent podcast for people who want to think more deeply about the world. Each week, we sit down with insiders, experts, and people paying close attention to unpack the ideas, stories, and forces shaping modern life — from diplomacy and power to parenting, technology, art, and justice.

No hot takes. No agenda. Just honest, candid conversation — the kind that holds up after the news cycle moves on.
